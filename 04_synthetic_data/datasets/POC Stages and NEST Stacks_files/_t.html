Where the two meet

They meet at the stack. The POC logs each string, which is 4 stacks in series, so the string voltage divided by 4 gives the stack voltage at the same current as a NEST stack.

On that common footing the build, the rated current and the temperature line up. The voltage and the fuel do not.

Power

Power is voltage times current. At the same current a NEST stack gives about 10 % more voltage, so it also gives about 10 % more power. At the rated 30 A the 20 NEST stacks would make 67.2 kW, against 52.0 kW in the POC twin. As the cells age the power falls with the voltage: NEST stack 1 at 30 A goes from 3.35 to 3.21 kW over its 7,269 hours.
