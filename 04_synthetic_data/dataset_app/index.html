Stage counts

Every run starts with a cold start up and ends in shutdown. Part loads and cold shutdowns start and end in hot standby, so hot standbys follow from the other two counts. Rated power is reached only from a part load (at most one per part load) and then goes to hot standby. The plant never ran above 34 kW, so rated power readings (30 A per string, 52 kW) are extrapolated from part load (best fitting curve per sensor). The stack temperature comes from the twin heat balance and keeps rising at rated power (no temperature limits in the twin).
